[idaho_itd] USFS Airfield [shortfield] This Forest Services strip is located approximately 3 miles from Dixie Town, in a valley above the Salmon River Canyon. The strip can get busy with Forest Serviecs fire fighters in the summer and fall fires seasons. Camping: There is no camping available at the airstrip, but the Halfway House Campground is located on Crooked Creek, approximately 1/2 mile down stream. The campground road road on the south side of the runway. The Halfway House campground has designated sites, and a pit toilet.
