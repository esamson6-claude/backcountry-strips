[ubcp] POTATO RIDGE AIRSTRIP IS SEASONALLY CLOSED FROM MARCH 1 TO JULY 31 . Old airstrip with some nice canyon hiking & exploring nearby. Short and rough, but nice spot to get away to. Caution for Ferruginous hawks, which are federally protected raptors. Ferruginous hawks are the largest hawks in North America, with a wing span of up to 5 feet. The hawks are ground-nesting birds which are known to breed and inhabit the open, arid scrub environment in the Potato Ridge Airstrip area. Please avoid disturbing these beautiful birds.
